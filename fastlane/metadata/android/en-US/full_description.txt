<p><b>chut</b> (French for "shh!") checks every unknown call <b>before your phone rings</b> and silences telemarketing. Free, open source, with no account, no ads, no trackers and no internet access. Every feature and every statistic, for everyone.</p>
<p><b>How it filters</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Your block list, if you want one: block a number, or import your phone's list; whole ranges (0162*) too</li>
<li>The 12 number ranges ARCEP reserves for telemarketing in France (01 62, 09 48…)</li>
<li>A model learned on the phone from what you block: it recognises numbers that look like the ones you block (same number blocks, same hours). Its accuracy is measured by cross-validation and shown in the app.</li>
<li>Your allow list and your contacts always ring</li>
</ul>
<p>Doubtful calls ring silently with a "Block / Not spam" notification; every tap retrains the model. Every decision keeps its reasons.</p>
<p><b>All the statistics</b>: per day, week, type, reason, region, hour and weekday, the most insistent numbers, time saved and a weekly report.</p>
<p><b>Senior protection</b>: one switch and only contacts ring normally.</p>
<p>Works with the app closed or after a restart. In French by default, or in English. Android 10 or newer. Licensed under the GNU GPL v3 or later.</p>
